# Commercial evaluation — honest release candidate

**Launchpad — Your Career, Higher.** organizes international career goals, learning plans, employer research and application follow-through. Guest mode offers an immediate local demonstration. Verified accounts, private cloud workspaces and optional consent-based AI are prepared for owner-configured services.

A credible demonstration can show the actual tracker, Kanban stages, directory/favorites, editable personal roadmap, tasks, reminders, real-data analytics and data export. Label sample entries explicitly. Show unavailable integrations honestly. No testimonials, user counts, partnerships, endorsements, hiring status, revenue or success metrics are fabricated.

Feedback is collected through the account's owned `feedback` table, or downloaded as JSON by guests. The operator must establish a real review/support process before promising response times. No invented support address is shown.

No checkout, subscription, recurring charge, paid tier or payment processor is implemented. Before charging customers, determine the legal operator, target jurisdictions, support channels, vendor terms, provider licensing, pricing/cost model, taxes, refunds/cancellations and billing security. Add a real payment provider and verify webhook signatures/server-side entitlements in a separate reviewed implementation. Never gate access using a frontend-only payment flag.

The Privacy Policy and Terms in `legal/` are drafts. They explain actual behavior and pending operator/retention details; they do not certify compliance. Obtain qualified legal review, assess child privacy obligations, complete service-provider agreements, define retention/deletion/incident procedures and validate accessibility with human assistive-technology users before a public commercial launch.
