# Future buyer-readiness checklist

This is preparation for evaluation, not a valuation, offer, revenue claim or sale.

- [ ] Identify the legal owner/entity, source authorship and assignments. The
  repository does not by itself prove a complete IP chain. Review contributor
  contracts, brand/logo provenance and any employment/contractor restrictions.
- [ ] Retain an architecture/data-flow map: static frontend → Supabase Auth/RLS/
  functions → Paddle/OpenAI/Remotive/optional email. Keep vendor credentials out
  of buyer screenshots and public documentation.
- [ ] Inventory dependencies from the lockfile and `vendor/LICENSES.txt`; SDK and
  deployment tools have separate terms. Review actual license texts, notices and
  transfer obligations with counsel. System fonts and an authentic app screenshot
  do not imply endorsement by employers or third parties.
- [ ] Prove registrar/domain ownership, DNS/certificate/recovery control and
  transferability. A GitHub Pages username URL is not an owned commercial domain.
- [ ] Export verified subscription/event records by environment and reconcile
  them with Paddle settlements, refunds, discounts, taxes and bank receipts.
  Do not present sandbox or legacy grants as paid subscribers or cash revenue.
- [ ] Define MRR, active account, churn, cohorts and conversion denominators.
  Document historical coverage gaps; unavailable churn baselines remain unknown.
- [ ] Reconcile operating costs, actual model usage/invoices, SMTP, support,
  hosting/domain/legal costs and contribution estimates. No fabricated margins.
- [ ] Maintain CI/test evidence, real acceptance results, deployment IDs, migration
  history, rollback and restored-backup evidence. Record known security/privacy
  gaps and incident history honestly; do not claim an audit certification.
- [ ] Finalize privacy/Terms/refund/retention policies, processor agreements,
  international transfer safeguards, rights requests and guardian/age procedures.
- [ ] Inventory service ownership, administrator access, MFA/recovery contacts,
  secret rotation, notification endpoints and least-privilege deployment tokens.
- [ ] Confirm Paddle merchant-of-record, Cloudflare, Supabase, AI and email account
  transfer/assignment rules and required vendor approvals. Do not assume customer
  payment methods or contracts can be moved by exporting database rows.
- [ ] Prepare an anonymized, access-controlled data room and legal diligence
  process. Do not expose customer records or signing secrets to prospective buyers.
- [ ] Document maintenance, release cadence, support ownership, monitoring,
  reconciliation duties and continuity if the original operator is unavailable.

A prospective buyer must independently verify financials, ownership, contracts,
security and customer permissions. No sale price, business valuation, revenue
figure, user count or acquisition interest is invented by this package.
