## Product description

Launchpad — Your Career, Higher. A focused workspace for international career
planning: track applications, research employers, plan learning milestones and
review progress. Start locally with JSON backups. Configured paid accounts add
protected cloud editing and limited, consent-based generative career coaching.
Live jobs show retrieved sources; unavailable integrations say so explicitly.

Proposed Free/Pro/Premium pricing is maintained in `lib/plans.mjs`. Confirm prices,
provider tax presentation, allowances and legal terms before publishing offers.
Do not advertise priority access or tools that are not implemented.

## Support and cancellation response

Acknowledge the account's request without asking for passwords, card numbers or
emailed Auth tokens. Verify identity through the signed-in account/provider
receipt flow. Check actual provider status before confirming cancellation dates.
Explain period-end versus immediate access changes and the reviewed refund route.
Never claim a refund or cancellation occurred merely because a browser button
was clicked. Escalate uncertain operations according to OPERATIONS.md.

## 30-day checklist (dates move if release gates fail)

- Days 1–3: owner/legal/vendor review; verify entity, support, domain and consent.
- Days 4–6: separate sandbox/staging projects, backups and SMTP/DNS setup.
- Days 7–9: real mailbox, two-user RLS, import/export/deletion acceptance.
- Days 10–12: sandbox checkout, signed deliveries, renewals, plan changes, failures,
  cancellation, expiry, replay and unauthorized-owner tests with official test methods.
- Days 13–15: real AI/token budgets, source jobs, physical mobile/browser tests,
  accessibility review, hosted CI and restore/rollback rehearsal.
- Days 16–18: invite a small consenting early-access group; disclose RC limitations.
- Days 19–21: review qualitative feedback, fix actual blockers, inspect support load.
- Days 22–24: verify proposed prices/credits against observed cost and customer value.
- Days 25–27: if every gate passes, obtain explicit owner approval for live merchant
  activation; perform an authorized minimal live acceptance under the reviewed policy.
- Days 28–30: publish truthful launch content and continue small organic outreach.
  Monitor actual consented funnel counts, support, invoices and AI spend. If gates
  fail, remain in early access; do not force a paid launch to meet a calendar.
