Recurring subscriptions

A subscription renews monthly until canceled. Pro/Premium access depends on verified provider records and server limits. Browser checkout events do not activate access. AI request credits reset each UTC calendar month and count submitted attempts, including provider errors; daily and platform cost limits also apply. Unused credits do not roll over.

Changes and cancellation

Plan changes take effect immediately using provider-calculated proration after you review the preview and explicitly confirm. Cancellation at period end stops renewal; immediate cancellation ends paid access after provider confirmation. Existing data remains exportable. Free accounts cannot create or edit paid cloud collections after access ends.

Refunds and payment issues

Contact the operator using the published support address or the contact route on your Paddle receipt. The operator must publish a concrete refund procedure and applicable time limits before sales. Statutory consumer rights, cooling-off periods, local law and Paddle's applicable buyer terms must be reviewed and honored; this draft does not waive them or promise automatic refunds. Payment failures may suspend paid editing and AI until provider status is resolved.

Eligibility and deletion

Paid contracting is restricted to adults aged 18 or older. Do not subscribe on behalf of a minor without a legally reviewed guardian process and operator approval. Cancel active billing and wait for confirmed cancellation before deleting an account. Required financial records may remain with the provider and operator under an approved retention schedule; deleting application data does not erase legally required invoices.
